1 VARIANT
1. N+1 Optimization:
ProductListModelSerializerda har bir mahsulot uchun umumiy sevimlilar sonini (favorites_count) chiqaruvchi field qo‘shing.
Database so‘rovlari soni mahsulotlar soniga bog‘liq bo‘lmasligi (N+1 muammosi bo‘lmasligi) kerak.

2. Validation Logic:
UserRegisterModelSerializerda foydalanuvchi yoshi 18 dan kichik bo‘lsa,
ValidationError qaytaruvchi validate_birth_date metodini yozing.

3. Cart Security:
CartItemListAPIViewda foydalanuvchi faqat o‘ziga tegishli savatchadagi mahsulotlarni ko‘rishi uchun get_querysetni to‘g‘ri filtrlang.

4. Custom Field:
CartItemModelSerializerda har bir element uchun umumiy narxni (total_price = quantity * product.price) hisoblab beruvchi SerializerMethodField yarating.

5. Filtering:
Product API uchun mahsulot nomi (name) bo‘yicha qidiruv (SarchFilter) va narx bo‘yicha saralash (OrderingFilter) imkoniyatini qo‘shing.



2 VARIANT
1. Warehouse Logic:
CartItemModelSerializerda mahsulot qo‘shilayotganda Product.quantity (ombordagi soni) yetarli ekanligini tekshiring.
Agar miqdor yetarli bo‘lmasa, "Omborda mahsulot yetarli emas" xabarini qaytaring.

2. Custom Permission:
Faqat type = 'seller' bo‘lgan foydalanuvchilar mahsulot yaratishi uchun IsSellerUser nomli custom permission yarating va uni View’ga biriktiring.

3. Slug Update Logic:
SlugBaseModel dagi save metodini shunday o‘zgartiringki,
u faqat mahsulot yaratilayotganda emas, balki nomi tahrirlanib o‘zgarganda ham yangi slug yaratib bersin.

4. Nested Category:
CategoryModelSerializer ichida kategoriyaning ostidagi barcha sub-kategoriyalarni (children) rekursiv ko‘rinishda chiqaring.

5. District Filter:
DistrictListAPIView da viloyat ID-si (region_id) orqali filtrlashni django-filter yuborilgan parametrlar asosida sozlang.



3 VARIANT
1. Advanced Optimization:
CartItemListAPIView da select_related va prefetch_relateddan foydalanib, product,
seller va mahsulotning barcha rasmlarini jami 2-3 ta queryda yuklang.

2. Password Safety:
UserChangePasswordModelSerializerda yangi parol eski parol bilan bir xil bo‘lib qolmasligini tekshiruvchi validator yozing.

3. Ownership Permission:
Sotuvchi faqat o‘ziga tegishli mahsulotlarni tahrirlashi (patch) yoki o‘chirishi uchun IsProductOwner permission-ni yarating va Product ViewSet-ga qo‘shing.

4. Dynamic Fields:
ProductListModelSerializerdan foydalanib, foydalanuvchi so‘rovda yuborgan fieldlarini
(masalan: ?fields=id,name) dinamik ravishda qaytaruvchi mantiqni View’da qo‘llang.

5. Price Range Filter: ProductFilterSetda mahsulotlarni min_price va max_price (narx oralig‘i) bo‘yicha filtrlashni django-filter orqali amalga oshiring.



4 VARIANT
1. Image Processing:
ImageBaseModel dagi convert_img_to_webp metodini takomillashtiring:
u rasmni WebP ga o‘tkazgach, bazadan eski rasmni (original faylni) o‘chirib yuborsin.

2. Inventory Recovery:
Savatchadan mahsulot butunlay o‘chirilganda (CartItem.delete), o‘sha mahsulotning miqdori (quantity) Product modelida qaytadan oshishini (omborda tiklanishini) ta’minlang.

3. Seller Constraint:
SellerModelSerializerda validate metodi orqali bitta foydalanuvchi faqat bitta Seller (do‘kon) yarata olishi haqidagi cheklovni yozing.

4. Discount Filter:
Product API da faqat chegirmasi bor (discount > 0) bo‘lgan mahsulotlarni chiqaruvchi custom filter metodini (BooleanFilter) yozing.

5. Hidden Defaults:
SellerModelSerializerda owner maydoni foydalanuvchiga ko‘rinmasligi,
lekin yaratilayotganda avtomatik ravishda so‘rov yuborgan foydalanuvchi (request.user) biriktirilishini ta’minlang.
